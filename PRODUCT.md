# Product

<!-- impeccable:product-schema 1 -->

## Platform

web

## Users

Bachelor's and Master's students discovering opportunities and tracking applications, as specified in AGENTS.md.

## Product Purpose

InternCalling helps students browse opportunities, understand eligibility and profile matches, maintain their profile and track applications.

## Capabilities and Constraints

Next.js frontend and FastAPI business logic, backed by Supabase identity and persistence. The core experience works without optional AI. Employer sources remain authoritative. Do not invent opportunities, salary, qualifications, match scores or profile facts.

## Brand Commitments

Keep the existing name and logo and the palette specified in AGENTS.md. The user explicitly requested the light opportunity list, structured detail sections with icons and bold labels, and CV-style profile shown in their screenshots. The user rejected the previous dark frontend. English is the requested display language for all postings; hide internal ATS hashtags such as #li-hybrid and #li-ag4.

## Product Principles

- Make relevant facts easy to scan and compare.
- Keep source information and provenance available.
- Keep browsing and editing usable on mobile and desktop.
- Show unavailable information honestly.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

Preserve semantic HTML, visible keyboard focus, readable contrast, responsive layout and reduced-motion support as required by AGENTS.md.
